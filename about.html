02 / OUR STORY

Built on curiosity. Driven by possibility.

Who we are

Official Empire Legends (OEL) is a Zambian-founded technology and creative brand established by Michael Situmbeko, a Software Engineering student and developer.

OEL was built around a simple principle: ideas should not remain ideas. They should be explored, engineered, tested and developed into things people can actually use.

Our work spans digital platforms, software development, web and mobile technologies, interface design, intelligent systems, automation and digital media.

We are building OEL through practical work, technical growth and purposeful collaboration, with an ambition to contribute to Zambia's growing digital landscape.

03 / THE FOUNDER

Michael Situmbeko

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Michael founded Official Empire Legends with a vision of building a technology and creative brand through learning, engineering and original ideas.

His interests include software engineering, web technologies, artificial intelligence, computer vision, automation and digital product development.

OEL represents his commitment to turning knowledge into practical work and building something that can grow beyond an individual.
