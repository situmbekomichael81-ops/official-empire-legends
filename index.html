Who we are

More than a name.
A vision in progress.

OEL is an independent, founder-led technology and creative initiative originating in Zambia. It brings together software ideas, digital products, emerging technologies and original creative work under one identity.

Our Mission

To explore, design and develop useful digital solutions and creative products through continuous learning, practical engineering and purposeful innovation.

Our Vision

To grow OEL into a recognised technology and creative brand with meaningful projects, trusted collaborations and opportunities reaching beyond Zambia.

OEL communication & verification

To help protect yourself when dealing with someone claiming to represent OEL:

  • Verify contact details against the channels published on this website.
  • Ask for written project scope, pricing and deliverables.
  • Request an agreed written record of payments and receipts where applicable.
  • Contact the founder through the published channels if something seems suspicious.
OEL does not ask people to trust unofficial accounts or unknown representatives. This website is an official communication point for the founder-led OEL initiative; it does not itself guarantee that third-party messages or accounts are genuine.
